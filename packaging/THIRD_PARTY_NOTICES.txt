FFmpeg 9.0.2 (bin/avformat-63.dll, avcodec-63.dll, avutil-61.dll, swresample-7.dll)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
License: GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later.
         licenses/LGPL-2.1.txt, licenses/FFmpeg-LICENSE.md
Copyright (C) the FFmpeg developers. FFmpeg is a trademark of Fabrice Bellard.
Website: https://ffmpeg.org/

Built LGPL-only: no --enable-gpl, no --enable-nonfree. Source: the unmodified
release tarball

  https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz

built with the MSVC toolchain (Visual Studio 2022, /MD) and this configure
line:

  --toolchain=msvc --target-os=win64 --arch=x86_64 --enable-shared
  --disable-static --disable-debug --disable-programs --disable-doc
  --disable-htmlpages --disable-manpages --disable-podpages --disable-txtpages
  --enable-libass --enable-libdav1d --enable-ffnvcodec --enable-nvenc
  --enable-nvdec --enable-cuvid --disable-filter=gfxcapture
  --pkg-config-flags=--static --extra-cflags=-MD --extra-cxxflags=-MD

Built into the FFmpeg DLLs included here (static libraries from vcpkg):
  - dav1d 1.5.4 (avcodec)          BSD 2-Clause licenses/dav1d-COPYING.txt
  - zlib 1.3.2 (avcodec, avformat) zlib License licenses/zlib-LICENSE.txt
  - nv-codec-headers (avcodec)     MIT          licenses/nv-codec-headers-LICENSE.txt
    (NVIDIA's encode/decode headers; the driver is loaded at run time)
libass and its dependencies, also in that configure line, go into avfilter,
which Muninn does not use and which is not included.

The FFmpeg libraries are separate DLLs, and muninn.dll loads them by name.
You may replace them with your own build of FFmpeg 9.0.x (same major library
versions: avformat 63, avcodec 63, avutil 61, swresample 7). Muninn's source
is public, so you can also rebuild muninn.dll against another FFmpeg
(LGPL 2.1 section 6).
